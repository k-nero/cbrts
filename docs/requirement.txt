Cross-Border Racehorse Transport System

ACTORS
- Logistics Manager
- Transport Specialist
- Fleet & Route Coordinator
- Vehicle Driver / Escort
- Customer

RESPONSIBILITIES

Logistics Manager
 + Receive and approve domestic and international racehorse transport orders.
 + Draw up the overall plan for each shipment (choose the vehicle, airline, route and transit points).
 + Assign tasks to the Transport Specialist, the Coordinator and the Driver/Escort.
 + Handle and approve route changes or extra costs that arise in emergencies.
 + View reports on revenue, operating costs and trip-completion performance (on-time delivery).

Transport Specialist (paperwork & quarantine)
 + Maintain the catalog of health, quarantine and customs regulations for each country and border crossing.
 + Create and store a digital transport dossier for each horse (vaccination certificate, horse passport, export/import permits).
 + Track the approval progress of dossiers with the authorities (Ministry of Agriculture, Customs, quarantine agencies).
 + Notify customers and guide them to supply any missing legal documents before the departure date.

Fleet & Route Coordinator
 + Manage the list of specialized transport equipment (horse trucks, aircraft holds, transport stalls).
 + Build the optimal travel route, setting the rest stops, quarantine stations and refuelling stops.
 + Update manually, or track, the trip's progress leg by leg (Departed, Arrived at stop, Cleared customs, Delivered).
 + Adjust the schedule and send alerts when traffic incidents, bad weather or border congestion occur.

Vehicle Driver / Escort
 + View the trip schedule, the horses in their charge and the assigned rest stops.
 + Manually report the trip status at each checkpoint (Departed, Arrived at stop, Customs clearance completed).
 + Keep a log of each horse's condition throughout the trip (Stable health, Off feed, Stressed, Climate change) with text and photos.
 + Send emergency incident reports (vehicle breakdown, prolonged traffic jam, horse medical incident) to the operations center.

Customer (racing club / horse owner)
 + Create a racehorse transport booking request (origin/destination, time, number of horses, special requirements).
 + Upload the horses' background papers and medical records as the transport provider requires.
 + Follow the trip's location and leg-by-leg status updates in real time.
 + Get notified when the horses have cleared customs or arrived safely at the handover point.

FLOWS

Required
 Flow 1: Transport Request Creation & Approval
 Flow 2: Legal Documents & Quarantine Clearance Management
 Flow 3: Route Planning & Vehicle Dispatch
 Flow 4: Status Updates & Trip Log

Optional
 Flow 5: Incident Handling & Emergency Rerouting
 Flow 6: Trip Handover & Acceptance
